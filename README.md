## Hi there 👋

I'm Glauco Roschel, a Cloud Engineer obsessed with Cloud Intrastructure, Windows Linux, and more.

Professional with over 10 years of experience in IT Infrastructure and Cloud Engineering, helping companies adopt tools that align with their business, understanding their needs, and promoting the adoption of cloud computing. This increases their productivity and creates modern, resilient, and highly available corporate environments.

Currently specialising in Advanced Cloud Architecture with Microsoft Azure, I am deepening my expertise in designing secure, resilient, and cost-optimised cloud environments. My focus includes enterprise landing zones, governance frameworks, identity modernisation, Zero Trust principles, high-availability architectures, infrastructure automation, and cloud-native security strategies, aligning cloud adoption with long-term business and digital transformation objectives.
